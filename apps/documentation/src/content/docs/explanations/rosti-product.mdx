# Rösti

SaaS multi-club session manager.

## Core concepts

- **Club** = Better Auth Organization (`owner` immutable, `admin`, `member`). A player can belong to several clubs. The app shows one club at a time. After signing in, a player in several clubs picks one. They can switch from the sidebar or the mobile header. Reopening the app returns to the last club.
- **Season** — stats window. A club has one active season at a time. Admins create, rename, and close seasons from the seasons page. Home stats and new matches follow the active season.
- **Match / MatchSeries** — single or recurring sessions with capacity
- **Attendance** — present / absent / pending
- **Lineup** — blue / red
- **Score** — set by club admins on the match; independent of player stats
- **Stats** — goals & assists per match (indicative only), aggregated per season. The home page shows at most two club award cards (the last match's elected player, or a prompt while that vote is open, plus one rotating award among top scorer, most wins, most losses, and most frequent teammates). The signed-in player's totals sit to the right of those cards once they have played.
- **Chat** — per-match messages with `@Name` mentions (stored as display names + `mentionedUserIds`)
- **Notifications** — email + push preferences. Joining a club only sends the team invitation. The registration email for the next match goes out a configurable number of days before kickoff (default 5). Admins can add one reminder for players who have not answered yet. Admins set how long a match lasts (default 90 minutes). The score reminder and the payment request go out 15 minutes after that end. Club admins also get a score reminder after a match ends. The players who were there get a push when the best-player vote opens, and another when the result is known.
- **Session fees** — club admins set a default pitch cost in settings. Saving it updates every upcoming match, and a new match uses that amount when no pitch cost is entered. After the session, Rösti splits the pitch plus any equipment cost across the players who were there. A player who brought a guest can be given two shares. Each player gets an email with a PayPal.me link that already includes their amount, marks the share paid in the app, and is reminded while it stays unpaid. The club chooses the reminder delays.

## Clients

- Desktop: `web-spa`
- Mobile: the same `web-spa`, installed as a PWA. Store apps are on standby (`apps/web-spa/capacitor.config.json`)
- Invite links: `web-spa` route `/invite/:invitationId`
